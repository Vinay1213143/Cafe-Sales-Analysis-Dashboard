# ☕ Cafe Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Cafe Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze café sales performance and uncover meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of revenue, profit, quantity sold, transactions, profit margin, product performance, and payment preferences. It enables users to monitor sales trends, identify top-selling products, and evaluate category-wise profitability through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall café sales and financial performance.
- Track daily sales trends over time.
- Identify top 5 selling items based on revenue.
- Compare revenue across different product categories.
- Analyze customer payment methods.
- Evaluate profit contribution by category.
- Provide interactive insights for better business decision-making.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Total sales revenue generated |
| Total Profit | Overall profit earned |
| Total Quantity | Total quantity of items sold |
| Total Transactions | Number of sales transactions |
| Profit Margin % | Profit as a percentage of revenue |
| Total Cost | Total cost associated with sales |

## ✨ Dashboard Features

### 1. Daily Sales Trends
Visualizes daily sales performance over time to identify fluctuations, peaks, and sales patterns.

### 2. Revenue by Category
Compares revenue generated by product categories such as Snacks, Beverages, and Desserts.

### 3. Top 5 Selling Items by Revenue
Highlights the five highest-revenue-generating items to identify popular and profitable products.

### 4. Revenue by Payment Method
Analyzes revenue distribution across payment methods, including Card, UPI, and Cash.

### 5. Total Profit by Category
Compares category-wise profit to understand which product categories contribute most to profitability.

### 6. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes slicers for:
- Category
- Payment Method
- Month Name
- Date Range

These filters allow users to explore sales performance across different periods and business categories.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Download and install Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Open the `.pbix` dashboard file.
4. Refresh the dataset if required.
5. Use the slicers and visualizations to explore sales performance.
